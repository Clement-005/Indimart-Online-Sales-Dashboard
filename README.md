# Indimart-Online-Sales-Dashboard
Designed and implemented interactive dashboards to analyze online sales performance across categories, states, and customer segments. The aim of this project was to provide actionable insights into revenue, profitability, and demand distribution.

The first dashboard provides a high‑level view of performance, showing GH₵437.77K in revenue, GH₵113.12K net profit, 500 orders, and 336 customers. Monthly trends highlight seasonal peaks, while category analysis reveals Electronics driving ~38% of revenue, Clothing ~33%, and Furniture ~29%, with balanced profit margins across all three. A geographic map further illustrates revenue distribution by state, guiding regional strategy. The second dashboard complements this by drilling into regional demand and product mix: Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh lead in orders, clothing sub‑categories dominate quantity sold, and a scatter plot confirms the link between sales volume and revenue. Category‑level data shows clothing as the largest driver of orders, while weekday analysis uncovers subtle buying patterns. Together, these dashboards demonstrate the ability to track profitability, identify growth opportunities, and align marketing and inventory decisions with customer behavior.
